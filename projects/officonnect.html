The Problems

Lack of efficient office schedule app

The interface of the original meeting schedule system is confusing.

Since the number of existing offices cannot meet the demand due to the increase in employees, the department needs a new and flexible office model.

The Solution

Efficient and clean is the key

Simple daily schedule

  • Sliding calendar streamlines task management.
  • Card-based design improves schedule's user-friendliness and visual appeal for intuitive task management
  • Use icons and colors for an intuitive taskbar visual instead of text.

Efficient and clean booking

  • Book public conference rooms and other facilities in office buildings in two easy steps
  • Use the notification system to notify everyone about the meetin
  • Avoid the embarrassment of two people using the same furniture at the same time.

Easy to create tasks and meeting

  • Quickly switch between individual tasks or create meetings
  • Group management of staff in different departments
  • Different groups have different icons, which is convenient for meeting initiators to identify.

User interview

Needs research is key

A comprehensive app design is crucial to address the challenges faced by UC Davis' traffic service department, including limited conference room availability and scheduling conflicts. The COVID-19 pandemic has amplified the need for remote or hybrid meetings, exacerbating these issues. To inform the app's design enhancements, a questionnaire survey was conducted among 23 employees, capturing key insights and data. The graph above visually represents the survey findings, emphasizing the importance of resolving conference room management challenges within the department.

The UCD transportation employee survey

User story:

I seek a flexible working approach for my employees amidst the changes caused by COVID-19. Hybrid meetings have become the norm, but conflicts arise due to limited office space. The current schedule system is chaotic, resulting in missed meeting times for field employees. Furthermore, due to limited office facilities, volunteers lack individual desks. I desire an app that can effectively manage schedules and provide information on office facility availability, ensuring efficient utilization of resources. Implementing such an app would improve coordination, productivity, and overall effectiveness in our dynamic work environment.

Goals:

  • An efficient conference room reservation system
  • Quick View List of Daily Tasks
  • Quickly complete the appointment of different meetings

Case study

  • Identify pain points: Analyze shortcomings to understand user frustrations and address them in your app, improving user satisfaction.
  • Enhance usability: Study usability issues to design a more intuitive interface, streamlined processes, and a visually appealing experience for users.
  • Improve features: Learn from competitors' app limitations to enhance features and provide a comprehensive and advanced set of functionalities in your app.

Design logic

Simplifying the use process is the key

  • Conference room reservation only needs two steps to complete.
  • Create meetings and personal tasks on the same page.
  • Meeting group categorization enables meeting initiators to shorten creation time and make it easier to manage.
