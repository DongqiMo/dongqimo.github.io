The Problems

Low usage of the students apartment's many recreational facilities is due to the events notification system and the complex app design, resulting in a poor living experience.

Key Insights

85% of students avoid apartment activities due to insufficient information channels.

70% struggle with the app's complex interface, especially on the maintenance page.

Goal

Design an student apartment management app with mild social features to boost awareness of activities, increase facility usage, and enhance user experience and design.

Solutions

01.

Add apartment events page

02.

Simplify the apartment maintenance reporting process

03.

Add illustrations and animations to enhance user experience

DESIGN SOLUTIONS

Events Page

  • An activity page in student apartments for users to create events, promoting closer ties and improving living experiences.
  • Using the reservation system can boost the use of entertainment amenities in student apartments.

Maintain page

  • The maintenance process is streamlined with a few simple steps and a user-friendly interface.
  • Incorporate illustrations and animations to improve the app's user experience.
