Hello! I’m Dongqi Mo, a UI/UX designer and researcher with an MFA in Design from the University of California, Davis. My work focuses on using XR technology to create innovative interactive experiences that enhance both everyday life and educational environments.

My research is driven by a commitment to integrating interdisciplinary knowledge spanning user experience design, data analysis, and computer graphics to advance XR technology in practical applications. I believe XR holds immense potential, not only for enhancing sensory experiences but also for building meaningful connections between digital and physical spaces.

In the future, I aim to continue pushing the boundaries of XR technology, delivering diverse, immersive interactive experiences that redefine how users engage with the digital world.

Research Project

“Safe ski” - Evaluation of Visual Alerts for Skiers on Slow-Speed Zones with Virtual Reality (2024) View

  • Developed an AR interface in a VR environment to provide real-time speed alerts, enhancing skier safety in slow zones.
  • Conducted tests with 14 volunteers of varying skiing experience; results confirmed AR's potential in reducing accidents.
  • Designed and tested various speed and warning icons using VR technology.

"Officonnect" - A new generation schedule management app designed for UCD transportation department (2023) View

  • Conducted comprehensive surveys and interviews with UCD Transportation staff to identify challenges in remote work and office management.
  • Designed a streamlined interface focused on task coordination and communication.

“Family” - A communication app tailored specifically for single-parent families that makes staying in touch easier (2020) View

  • Surveyed 20 single-parent families; over 50% of children reported a lack of companionship and communication.
  • Designed shared calendars, budgeting tools, and flexible communication features to address work-life balance and improve family communication.

“Comb” - A student apartment management software with social attributes (2020) View

  • Surveyed 60 student housing residents in Cedar City, Utah, revealing issues with low social activity participation and complex application interfaces.
  • Developed Comb to streamline maintenance tasks and enhance event creation and participation.
